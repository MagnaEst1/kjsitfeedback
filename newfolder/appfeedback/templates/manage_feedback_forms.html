Steps to configure Gmail App Password:

  2. Sign in to your Gmail account if prompted.
  3. Under App name, enter a descriptive name (e.g. KJSIT Feedback) and click Create.
  4. Google will display a 16-character App Password (e.g. xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx).
  5. Copy the 16-character password and paste it into the password field below (spaces will be stripped automatically).

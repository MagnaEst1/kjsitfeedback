After Creating Your Form

What you can do once your feedback form is created

Add Custom Questions

Add rating, text, or multiple-choice questions specific to your subject or teaching style.

Edit Default Questions

Modify the default questions to better suit your needs or remove ones that aren't relevant.

Activate Your Form

Toggle the form status to make it available for students to fill out when you're ready.

Reorder Questions

Drag and drop questions to arrange them in the order that makes most sense for your feedback form.

View Responses

Once students start submitting feedback, you can view and analyze all responses in detail.

Export Data

Download feedback responses as CSV or Excel files for further analysis or reporting.

Ready to Customize?

After creating your form, you'll be able to access the Questions Management page to customize all questions to your specific needs.

Manage Questions link will appear in your form actions

Important Information

Default questions will be automatically added to the form

For practical and tutorial subjects, you must select a practical batch

Students can only see forms for their assigned divisions and batches

For theory subjects, practical batch should be left empty

Forms can be edited after creation through the manage forms page
